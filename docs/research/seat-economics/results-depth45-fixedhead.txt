  READING THIS FILE -- what it settles and what it does NOT
====================================================================================================

  THE CONTROL PASSED, AND IT IS A KNOWN-ANSWER CONTROL RATHER THAN A SELF-CONSISTENCY ONE.
  The N=5 / decay row reproduces results-headsize.txt at c1 = 45% on FOUR independent numbers:
  back bounds 3777 / 1740 / 0 and the passive LP at +5.02% / +0.49% / -2.17%. At n = 5 the decaying
  ladder IS the shipped roster, 450 : 220 : 165 : 110 : 55. So this is the published harness.

  WHAT THIS INSTRUMENT MEASURES: the LOWER bound only -- the first phi at which EVERY back seat
  beats a passive pro-rata LP. It does not measure the front's ceiling.

  WHY THE FRONT'S CEILING NEVERTHELESS DOES NOT MOVE WITH N, derived from two measured facts:
    (a) the wing bar is the head's own alternative -- a keeper-managed ATM range holding the head's
        capital. The head holds 45% x $1,000,000 = $450,000 AT EVERY N in this file, so the bar is
        literally the same number.
    (b) r1 is INVARIANT IN N at fixed c1. Measured, phi = 9500:
            BENIGN  -4.86% -4.87% -4.88% -4.88%   across N = 5, 8, 16, 32
            NORMAL  -4.98% -4.99% -4.99% -5.00%
             TOXIC  -3.79% -3.80% -3.80% -3.80%
        The head's return depends on the head SHARE, not on how the rest of the book is subdivided.
    => phi_max is N-invariant, so results-headsize.txt's 7340 at c1 = 45% carries across.
    LABELLED: this is DERIVED from (a) and (b), not separately measured at each N.

  THE RESULT

    all-regime window at a FIXED 45% head, using phi_max = 7340 from the derivation above:

       N      DECAYING ladder                 EQUAL ladder
      ---------------------------------------------------------------------
       5      [3777, 7340]  NON-EMPTY         [4949, 7340]  NON-EMPTY
       8      [5120, 7340]  NON-EMPTY         EMPTY (toxic back bound 8943 > 7340)
      16      EMPTY (toxic: rank 2 never)     EMPTY (toxic: rank 2 never)
      32      EMPTY (toxic: rank 2 never)     EMPTY (toxic: ranks 2-3 never)

    In BENIGN and NORMAL every back seat clears at EVERY N tested, up to 32, under BOTH shapes.
    The only failure anywhere is the TOXIC regime, and it is ONE seat -- rank 2 -- missing by
    -0.273pp (decay, N=16), -0.141pp (decay, N=32), -0.136pp / -0.127pp (equal). Rank 2's turnover
    is 1.00 in every one of those cells, so it is a REAL fill-based miss and not the seat-32
    non-participation artefact.

  WHAT THIS OVERTURNS.  results-depth-basis.txt section 3 reports EMPTY windows at N = 8, 16 and 32
  and the documentation hardened that into "five is near the measured maximum". That sweep let the
  head share fall from 66.7% to 6.1% as N grew. Held at the shipped 45%:
    - EIGHT seats has a non-empty all-regime window on the shipped ladder shape. The old table said
      N=8 was EMPTY in BENIGN and TOXIC.
    - SIXTEEN and THIRTY-TWO seats work in BENIGN and NORMAL and miss on exactly one seat in TOXIC.
      The old table said ranks 2-5 and ranks 2-17 respectively failed.
  THE SEAT COUNT WAS NEVER THE BINDING CONSTRAINT. THE HEAD SHARE WAS.

  THE SHAPE MATTERS, AND ONLY AT THE EDGE.  N=8 clears in TOXIC on the decaying ladder (5120) and
  not on the equal one (8943). Reported rather than resolved: it is one cell, on the coarse
  6-point phi grid this file inherits, and a claim about N=8 should be re-run on a fine grid before
  anything is shipped on it.

  WHAT IS STILL NOT CLAIMED.  Nothing here says a 32-seat book is a good idea. The gas cost at that
  depth is the quadratic corner of the contract (2.57M / 2.64M for the two deposit extremes), a
  32-seat book at a 45% head gives the back seats ~1.8% of the book each, and MAX_SEATS = 32 remains
  a storage fact. What is now false is the claim that the ECONOMICS forbid it.
