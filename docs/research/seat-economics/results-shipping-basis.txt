====================================================================================================
5.  REGIME WEIGHTING -- should TOXIC drive the choice?
====================================================================================================
  Excluding TOXIC entirely picks a constant that is optimal only in good states, which
  is the error this project calls green-number-chasing. Weighting it by the CALENDAR it
  actually occupies is the honest middle: a band that dies in a day contributes a day.

      regime   mean life   calendar share if equally likely
      BENIGN       61.0d                             74.9%
      NORMAL       19.1d                             23.4%
       TOXIC        1.4d                              1.7%

  A TOXIC band is ~1-2% of the calendar even if a toxic regime is as LIKELY as a benign
  one, because it dies almost immediately. Its crossover therefore should not set the
  constant -- but it should not be deleted from the average either.
