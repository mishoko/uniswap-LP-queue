====================================================================================================
C.  THE WING LADDER, A PRIORI, PER RANK  (phi = 8500)
====================================================================================================
  Each rank's substitute is TWO plain positions -- one per direction -- over the price
  slices the funding geometry says that rank occupies, funded with the same capital and
  split in proportion to the seat's own two-sided exposure. Sized from FUNDING, never
  from observed fills: a wing sized from hindsight is a tautological witness.

  A TIE IS A LOSS. The wing is free, cancellable in any block, uncapped, needs no
  roster seat and lets the holder choose their own ticks. Matching it while charging
  rent and adding contract risk is losing, so 'seat > wing' is reported STRICTLY.

  NOTE ON THE NULL: these per-rank wings do NOT tile the band (they are sized to
  slices that overlap at the head and leave gaps), so there is no built-in zero-sum
  identity for the LADDER. The identity that IS enforced is the one that has one:
  sum(seats) == the pro-rata position, checked at 1e-15 in results-tranche.txt section 11,
  and the full-band wing null, re-checked here.

  full-band wing null over 2160 runs: worst fee 6.81e-03, worst markout 9.45e-04

  THE ARITHMETIC, AND WHICH PARTS ASSUME ANYTHING.

   A(phi) = rank1 realised  -  max(pro-rata LP, best managed wing)   [ALL MEASURED]
            the front's advantage per dollar over the best thing it could do instead.

   Participation, no assumption:  a holder pays rent tau*P over the life and will not
   pay more than the advantage is worth, so
        P_max = A(phi) * C * (1yr/life) / tau        and
        rent collected over the life = tau * P * (life/1yr)  <=  A(phi) * C.

   ****  THE RENT THE BOOK CAN COLLECT IS BOUNDED BY A(phi) AND IS INDEPENDENT OF tau.
         tau sets the PRICE LEVEL of a seat, not the REVENUE. Raising tau lowers the
         self-price by exactly the same factor.  ****

   Only the SPLIT of that bound between holder and book depends on an assumption. With
   the Harberger equilibrium the project's README already flags as unestablished,
   P* = A/(tau+k), the book gets A * tau/(tau+k) -- a fraction, never more than A.
