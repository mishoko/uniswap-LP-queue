16. phi* -- SOLVED, NOT SWEPT. The participation constraint at rank 1.
====================================================================================================
  The constraint: rank 1's queue-net equals what the SAME capital earns as an ordinary
  pro-rata LP. Two independent routes, and they are a check on each other:

   NUMERIC  -- interpolate the measured rank-1 realised return across the phi grid and
               find where it crosses the measured pro-rata return. No assumptions.
   CLOSED   -- phi* = 1 - (rank 1's EXCESS DRAG over pro-rata)/(its EXCESS FEES over
               pro-rata), from the panel's displacement algebra. This assumes rank 1
               receives its PRO-RATA share of the premium pot, which it does not (it is
               the most-drained seat and carries the least standing weight), so the two
               are expected to DISAGREE and the size of the gap is the point.

18. THE MATCHED-WING CONTROL -- is a seat replicable with plain Uniswap liquidity?
====================================================================================================
  Read wing.py's header for the construction. Two caveats stated up front, neither
  resolved here because neither can be:
   (i) The wing is a PRICE TAKER against the tape the QUEUE pool produced. It does not
       feed its own depth back into the price path.
  (ii) A wing OVERLAPPING the band is not available inside this pool at all --
       `beforeAddLiquidity` refuses it. The substitute exists only in a different,
       unhooked pool of the same pair, which would have its own flow. Read the wing
       comparison as the arithmetic it is, not as an executable trade in THIS pool.
 (iii) The managed wing is OPTIMISTIC: it re-mints instantly at the post-swap price with
       no latency, no missed blocks and no MEV on its own conversion trade. A comparator
       biased in the WING's favour losing is strong evidence; winning is weaker.
