3.  CONTROL -- conservation: the queue book IS the undivided pool
====================================================================================================
  For each path:   residual = | sum_i (capital_i/CAPITAL) * ret_i  -  passive LP ret |
  The left side is the queue's 5 seats; the right side is a SEPARATE one-seat simulation
  with no premium at all.  Same seed, same price path.

  THE BRIEF ASKED FOR A ~1e-13 GATE.  That threshold is NOT met by this instrument and
  never has been, for two reasons that are in the model rather than in the arithmetic:
    (a) CLAMPING (sim.py:144) -- the 5-seat book's availability runs from its cursor, so a
        swap the queue clamps and the monolith does not moves the POOL price differently,
        and every later arb is then sized against a different spot.
    (b) THE HOLD RULE (sim.py:220-232) -- a pot with no eligible recipient is HELD.  Premium
        still held at path end sits inside the queue book and outside the LP's.
  results-headsize.txt section 9 measured this over its whole grid at up to 3.08e-04 and
  called it PASS.  The distribution is printed rather than a bound asserted.

6.  WHERE EACH SEAT'S EDGE COMES FROM -- an IDENTITY, not an inference
====================================================================================================
  sim.py's tie_out() asserts  opening + markout + own-fee + premium == closing balance,  so
  per-seat P&L splits EXACTLY into three legs and the edge over the LP splits into two:

      edge  =  ACTIVITY  +  COUPON
      ACTIVITY = (markout + own fee retained) / held  -  LP return
      COUPON   = premium received / held

  This is the number that settles 'does the seat earn or does it abstain', and it replaces
  an earlier draft of this file that tried to read the answer off the idle-path means.
  THAT DRAFT WAS WRONG: it labelled s5 'capital preservation', and s5 beats the LP by
  ~+2.96pp on its IDLE paths in BENIGN, a regime where the LP EARNS +5.02%.  Doing nothing
  cannot produce that.  s5 is being PAID, and the split below says so in one column.

  A seat with ACTIVITY < 0 and a positive edge is COUPON-FUNDED: it is not out-trading the
  LP, it is being subsidised by the seats in front.  That is the mechanism working as
  designed -- but it is NOT 'this seat beats an LP', and the two must not be conflated.

7b.  THE MECHANISM IS EXACTLY ZERO-SUM -- the back's gain IS the head's loss
====================================================================================================
  Section 3 already proved the queue book and the undivided pool are the same position, so
  this is forced rather than surprising -- but it is the sentence a funder needs, and it is
  printed in DOLLARS because a percentage hides it.  QUEUE does not create return.  It
  moves return from the seat at the front to the seats behind it.

  The `sum $` column is the section-3 conservation residual in dollars, and it is the
  undistributed premium still HELD at path end -- not slippage in the accounting.

  CORROBORATION FROM THE phi = 0 CONTROL (section 4), which is a DIFFERENT measurement:
  strip the premium and the back seats collapse.  In BENIGN at phi = 0 the passive LP
  returns +5.02% while s2..s5 return +0.02%, +0.44%, +0.07%, -0.00% --
  every one of them 4.6-5.0pp BELOW the LP.  Their entire case rests on the coupon.
