THE HEAD-SHARE SWEEP -- does a bigger head open a phi window in ALL THREE REGIMES?
====================================================================================================
  PREM_WEIGHT = 'liquidity_excl', the basis the contract implements (QueueHook.sol 1150/1154/1494).
  N in (5,) held FIXED; the HEAD's share of the book is the only thing that varies.
  Seats 2..N always split (1 - c1) in the project's own LINEAR proportion (4:3:2:1 at N=5).
  $1,000,000 book, band +/-10%, fee 0.30%, retail 15/hr, 4 disjoint seed ranges x 30 = 120 paths per cell.
  phi swept 0-9500 step 500 for all crossovers; phi = 5100 measured
  SEPARATELY for the gap/spread statistics and deliberately NOT inserted into the
  crossover grid (see the header).
  8 worker processes (a mutation campaign owned the rest of the machine).

  *** EVERY WINDOW IN THIS FILE IS RENT-FREE. ***  sim.py models no Harberger rent at all
  (verified: its only 'rent' match is the substring in 'CURRENT'), while the deployed hook
  charges Harberger rent on each seat's self-price -- reported as RENT_BPS = 1_000 per
  RENT_PERIOD, attributed not verified, since src/ was off-limits to this author during a
  live mutation campaign -- and -- as of this session -- pays it FORWARD, from
  the back seats to the front.  That is a second transfer channel running OPPOSITE to the
  premium.  These windows are therefore a LOWER bound on what the front can bear and an
  UPPER bound on what the back keeps.  A back seat clearing the LP by less than its rent
  bill has not really cleared it.  This file does not model it and does not guess at it.

  The 18/6 caveat also stands: the shipped pool's token0 premium is separately known to be
  inert (PITFALLS 5.124), so every premium number here is an upper bound on the contract's.

8b.  THE CLOSED FORM -- why the head share is the lever, algebraically
====================================================================================================
  PITFALLS 5.178 records the one quantity immune to the premium-basis question:

      the back's excess per unit of back capital  =  c1 * (LP - r1) / (1 - c1)

  It follows from sum_i c_i r_i = LP -- the premium is a transfer INSIDE the book, so it
  cannot change the pool's total -- and therefore holds under ANY weighting.

  READ THIS AS ALGEBRA, NOT AS EVIDENCE.  It is a rearrangement of the SAME identity that
  section 9 checks, so the agreement below is guaranteed by section 9 passing and could not
  read FAIL for any reason section 9 would not already have caught.  LAW 5: a control whose

  printed because it EXPLAINS the mechanism -- the factor c1/(1 - c1) goes from 0.50 at
