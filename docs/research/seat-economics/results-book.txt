2.  NET CONVERSION -- the load-bearing number
====================================================================================================

  conv/cap  = (a0_i - o0_i) * Pt / capital_i.  The seat's NET change in token0 holdings
              over the path, valued at the mark, as a fraction of the capital it put in.
  vs prorata= that same quantity divided by the POOL's net conversion per unit of capital,
              which is exactly what a pro-rata LP of the same size gets.  1.00 = the
              ordering rule delivered NOTHING.
  drain     = tk0_i / (token0 ever received).  a0 falls ONLY via tk0, so drain == 0 is
              EXACTLY the monotone-accumulator claim.  MONO% = share of the 120 paths on
              which the seat's token0 never once decreased.

  A NOTE ON THE RATIO, AND IT IS THE SAME TRAP AS SECTION 5.  conv/cap divided by the
  pool's own conv/cap is the natural statistic and it is UNUSABLE in BENIGN and NORMAL:
  those regimes are driftless, the pool's net conversion passes through zero, and a ratio
  whose denominator crosses zero is noise.  The headline is therefore the EXCESS -- a
  difference, always defined.  The ratio is printed only on the paths where the pool
  converted at least 10% of capital, with N stated.

  reads FAIL if: the reversed walk fails to concentrate conversion at the ends -- the
  'EXCESS' column then sits at 0.0000 for every rank and the mechanism is a no-op.  The
  numerator is per-rank and ordering-dependent; the denominator is the pool total, which is
  fixed by the price path and is bit-identical between modes (section 1a).  They are not
  the same quantity.

====================================================================================================
5.  EXECUTION QUALITY -- the number that decides the product
====================================================================================================

  A DISCARDED METRIC, RECORDED BECAUSE IT IS THE EXACT LAW 5 SHAPE.  The first draft of this
  section reported a NET price, -(a1_i - o1_i)/(a0_i - o0_i): the price of rank 0's net
  position change.  It is economically the right question and it is numerically worthless.
  Net conversion is a DIFFERENCE of two large numbers and goes through zero on any path that
  round-trips, so the denominator is dust and the ratio explodes.  Measured: it printed a
  mean net price of 8.48 against a TWAP of 1994.13, and 'rank 0 beats a taker by 9715 bps
  (SE 6318)'.  A 97% edge is not a finding, it is a divide-by-almost-zero, and the SE being
  two thirds of the mean is the tell.  It is reported below ONLY on the paths where the net
  conversion is at least 25% of the seat's opening token0, with N stated.

  THE WELL-CONDITIONED FORM.  Buy and sell legs separately.  Both denominators are GROSS
  quantities traded, which are large whenever the seat trades at all:
    buy  VWAP = numeraire paid / token0 received, INCLUDING the fee the seat retains and
                net of premium forfeited.  LOWER is better.
    sell VWAP = numeraire received (same basis) / token0 given up.  HIGHER is better.
    spread    = sell VWAP - buy VWAP, in bps of TWAP.  This is the market-maker's actual
                round-trip edge and it is what front-first pricing is supposed to buy.
    TWAP      = hourly mean of the EXTERNAL price over the band's life -- built from the
                price process, never from a fill, so it cannot move with the ordering rule.
    vs taker  = against a taker doing the same side clip-by-clip and paying FEE:
                buy  leg: 1e4*(TWAP*(1+FEE) - buyVWAP)/TWAP   POSITIVE = seat better
                sell leg: 1e4*(sellVWAP - TWAP*(1-FEE))/TWAP  POSITIVE = seat better

  reads FAIL if: front-first pricing hands rank 0 the stale end of every move.  It can and
  does read negative -- results-exec.txt already measured the head's per-fill edge at -5 to
  -27 bps under the shipped rule.

7.  VERDICT
====================================================================================================

  DOES THE TWO-ENDED BOOK DELIVER A MONOTONE PRIORITY LIMIT ORDER THAT BEATS A TAKER?

                                    NO.  Not in any regime, and not for either reason.

  1. IT IS NOT MONOTONE.  Rank 0's token0 never decreases on 27.5% of BENIGN paths, 28.3%
     of NORMAL, 0.0% of TOXIC and 96.7% of TOXIC-DN.  Read that row again: monotonicity is
     pinned by the DRIFT, not by the ordering rule.  Rank 0 is a monotone accumulator
     exactly when the price falls -- i.e. exactly when accumulating is the losing side --
     and never when it rises.  A limit buy you cannot stop working when the market runs
     away from you is not a limit buy, it is adverse selection with extra steps.

  2. IT LOSES ON PRICE.  Rank 0's buy VWAP goes from 1984.07 (SHIPPED) to 2048.40 (BOOK)
     against a TWAP of 1989.66 -- NORMAL, phi=0.  Against a taker buying the same clip by
     clip and paying the 0.30% fee, BOOK's rank 0 is -269 bps (NORMAL), -319 bps (BENIGN)
     and -342 bps (TOXIC-DN).  A mandated buyer under BOOK is WORSE OFF THAN A TAKER, by
     roughly three tenths of a percent to a third of a percent, in every driftless or
     falling regime.  It is +95 bps in TOXIC only, and section 5's mirror control shows
     that sign is the drift talking.

  3. THE U-SHAPE IS NOT THERE -- IT IS INVERTED.  Claimed: high turnover at both ends, low
     in the middle.  Measured (BENIGN, phi=0, turnover/capital): SHIPPED 149.7 / 2.7 / 1.5
     / 0.9 / 0.1, a clean monotone decline.  BOOK 36.1 / 90.3 / 55.7 / 26.3 / 4.2 -- a HUMP
     with its peak at rank 1.  Reading the book from both ends does not idle the centre, it
     makes the centre the only place BOTH directions can be served, so the centre churns.
     End/middle turnover ratio: 44.79 under SHIPPED, 0.35 under BOOK.

  4. THE MIDDLE'S MARKOUT GETS WORSE, NOT BETTER.  NORMAL, phi=8500: rank 1 markout goes
     -0.96% -> -4.40%, rank 2 -0.51% -> -3.16%.  That follows directly from (3): markout
     is what turnover buys you.

  5. WHO PAYS.  The identity holds to 0.0000, so rank 0's gain is somebody's loss and
     section 6 names them.  NORMAL, phi=8500: rank 0 +4.25pp, rank 1 -3.09pp, rank 2
     -2.45pp, rank 3 -0.81pp, rank 4 +0.06pp.  THE MIDDLE PAYS THE HEAD.  That is the exact
     opposite of the proposal's stated intent, which was to pay the middle from both ends.

  6. IT BREAKS THE CONSTRAINT phi = 8500 EXISTS TO SATISFY.  Share of (path, rank) cells in
     ranks 1-4 clearing the pro-rata LP, at the shipped phi: BENIGN 80.0% -> 37.5%, NORMAL
     86.5% -> 37.9%, TOXIC 75.8% -> 0.0%.  There is no phi that repairs this, because the
     premium is what was lifting the back over the bar and BOOK reroutes the premium TO THE
     HEAD: rank 0 ends up holding the token0 that the token1-direction premium is weighted
     by, so it collects the coupon it is supposed to be paying.  NORMAL phi=8500, premium
     received: rank 0 2.344% -> 2.922%, ranks 1-3 all DOWN.

  7. NO GAS SAVING EITHER.  Total seat-fills per path are within 1% between the two rules;
     BOOK redistributes the work, it does not reduce it.

  WHAT ACTUALLY KILLED IT, IN ONE SENTENCE.  Rank 0 already drains FIRST in the
  zeroForOne direction under the shipped rule, so reversing the OTHER direction changes
  nothing about how rank 0 BUYS -- it only stops rank 0 SELLING.  The whole economic effect
  is therefore 'the head holds its token0 longer', which is a LONG-DELTA TILT and not a
  queue-position product: it pays +4.49pp in TOXIC (drift +0.35) and -1.28pp in TOXIC-DN
  (drift -0.35), at phi=0, on 120 paths each, t = +61.96 and -20.10.  A mechanism whose
  sign is set by the drift is a directional bet wearing a market-structure costume, and it
  is sold to a seat holder who cannot choose the drift.

  ONE THING THE BOOK DOES DO, STATED SO IT IS NOT LOST.  Rank 0's round-trip spread widens
  from 41.1 to 179.2 bps (NORMAL, phi=0) because its inventory now cycles at the top of the
  band.  It is real and it is not enough: turnover falls 60.7 -> 12.3, so fee income falls
  9.11% -> 1.84% of capital while markout only improves -6.92% -> -2.11%.  The head pays
  7.27pp of fees to save 4.82pp of markout.  That is the trade, and it is a bad one.
