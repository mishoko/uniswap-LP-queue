## 1. v4 platform facts that bite

## 4. Proven-impossible — settled dead, do not re-litigate

| # | The idea | Why it is impossible | Status | Pointer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | **Detect toxic flow in a v4 hook** | Every readable signal is forgeable or invisible: `sqrtPriceLimitX96` (sentinel, free to forge); `amountOutMinimum` (**the hook cannot see it** — enforced in the router after the last callback); exact-in/out sign (free); priority fee (zero under private orderflow); `tx.origin`/EOA (sender is the router); trade size (splitting lemma); address/history (value moves between addresses inside one unlock for **52,700 gas**) | PROVEN | PLAN §E.19; archive §5.19; `archive/2026-08-26/docs/research/IDEAS_CONSTRAINED.md` §1 |
| 4.2 | **A curve that reduces LVR** | `ℓ(σ,p) = ½σ²p²·\|x'(p)\|` and marginal depth `d(p) = p²·\|x'(p)\|` are **the same quantity**: `ℓ = ½σ²·d`, for every invariant at every price (verified by recovering `σ²V/8` for constant product from the depth side). Halving LVR halves depth by the same factor | PROVEN (theorem) | PLAN §E.19; archive §5.22; `IDEAS_CURVES.md` §0 |
| 4.3 | **A hook that sees, gates, refunds or pays the trader** | `sender` is the router, `hookData` is caller-supplied, the recipient is taken after the last callback. Only three escapes exist — be the periphery (kills aggregator routing), a per-trade issuer signature (an off-chain censor on the swap path), or the token enforces it (ERC-3643/1400, which makes the hook redundant). **Uniswap Labs shipped escapes 1 and 3 with a forked router + forked posm; their hook is the redundant half** | PROVEN | PLAN §E.8, §E.19; archive §5.16/§5.18 |
| 4.4 | **Any mechanism keyed on a per-swap / per-address magnitude** (the splitting lemma) | Split the quantity into k parts at a cost of (k−1)×marginal gas — fractions of a cent. Directory: `circuit break\|price band\|rate.?limit\|max.{0,6}impact` → **6 built, 0 prized** | PROVEN | PLAN §E.15; archive §5.17 |
| 4.5 | **Any mechanism anchored on a per-block reference** | Waiting one block boundary costs **200 ms** on Unichain. Also: reflexivity-immunity and the cross-block exemption are the **same knob** — a reference an attacker cannot reset also cannot exempt the honest arb (the EMA variant closed the cross-block route at 7.2% of LP P&L) | PROVEN / MEASURED | PLAN §B.12; archive §5.19-20 |
